Email privacy & your choices

Updated October 23, 2026.

The free training list

When you sign up, we use your email to send a confirmation link. You join the training list only after confirming. The list is for occasional automotive training tips and Ask The Finance Guy product updates. Signing up is free and does not start a paid subscription.

What is stored and why

Our email provider, Resend, processes your email to deliver messages and maintain subscription status. A confirmation link contains signed, encoded email information; treat it as private and avoid forwarding it. We use your subscription information to operate this list, not to sell your email address.

Unsubscribe or request removal

Use the unsubscribe link on your confirmation page or in future training emails. You can also use our contact form to request removal or ask about your information. An unsubscribe record may be retained to prevent further marketing emails. Purchase and support messages are separate from the training list.

Website and purchases

The website uses Vercel Web Analytics to understand aggregate visits and interactions. We do not attach your email address to analytics events. Stripe handles checkout and payment details; the website does not collect your card number. The contact form sends the information you submit to our support inbox.

Need help?

Contact Ask The Finance Guy. Do not send passwords, Social Security numbers or card details.
